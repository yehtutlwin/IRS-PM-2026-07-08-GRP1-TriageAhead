Rulegen Review

One schema, one set of gates, seven drafting configurations run over the ESI v5 handbook — and a concept inventory that collapses every draft, including our own reviewed rulebook, into one list to review. Nothing here has entered rules/criteria.yaml; every concept below is a candidate until a person checks it against the cited page.

What the runs showed

  • The gates hold for every drafter. Across 409 rulegen drafts there were zero span failures — every quoted span was found verbatim on the cited page — and every page attribution in the whole-document and agentic runs was correct. The source-first protocol is enforceable by code, regardless of model or mode.
  • A stronger model finds more and obeys less. Pro re-found the most reviewed rules (39 of 47) and produced the most new candidates, but invented 17 field names — per-complaint booleans like complaint.chest_pain in place of the enumerated complaint.category — and labelled a third of its drafts operationalized without saying what it operationalized. Flash stayed inside the vocabulary. Review load scales with capability.
  • An agent's self-report is not evidence. Two of three Pro agentic runs claimed to have written 34 and 70 entries; no file existed. The transcript is the record, so transcripts are now kept per run.
  • Keep the workspace clean — that is the whole isolation requirement. The agent did look around when it could (ls /tmp, env, find /); it never reached anything carrying answers, and results were identical with a stricter namespace. A fresh workspace with no prior drafts in reach is enough.
  • Agents self-verify when allowed. The Pro agent wrote its own schema and span checkers and grepped the pages for exact phrases before quoting them — the payoff of a rich environment, visible as 52 of 52 spans verified.
  • The modes are complementary. Whole-document reading under-generates but sees figures and tables that text extraction mangles (the algorithm box, the pediatric fever table); per-page over-covers prose; agentic runs cross-check. Union, dedupe through the concept graph, then review.
